How to make Maggi

  1. Gather all the ingredients: Maggi noodles, water, and the tastemaker.
  2. Open the Maggi noodles packet.
  3. Boil water in a pot.
  4. Add the Maggi noodles to the boiling water.
  5. Cook for 2-3 minutes until the noodles are soft.
  6. Drain the water and add the Maggi tastemaker.
  7. Mix well and serve hot.
