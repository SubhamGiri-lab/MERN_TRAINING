Resturant Menu

Soft Drinks

Soft drinks are non-alcoholic, flavored, and typically carbonated beverages. The term "soft" distinguishes them from "hard" liquors. They are made from a base of water, sweeteners (sugar or artificial substitutes), and flavorings.

Sandwiches

Sandwiches are categorized by how they are served (hot vs. cold) and their physical structure. The most popular types include classic closed sandwiches, flatbread wraps, open-faced varieties, and grilled or toasted favorites.

Pizza & Burger

Pizzas are baked flatbreads with a crispy or chewy dough base, typically covered in tomato sauce and melted cheese. Burgers consist of a grilled patty inside a soft sliced bun, layered with condiments and fresh toppings.
